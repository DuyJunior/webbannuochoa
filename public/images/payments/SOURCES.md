- `napas247-mark.png`: https://minisiteb.qltns.mediacdn.vn/minisite/napas/assets/image/service-logo-1.png (NAPAS 247 service mark linked from https://napas.com.vn/).
- `bidv-mark.png`: https://cdn.vietqr.io/img/BIDV.png (bank logo returned by https://api.vietqr.io/v2/banks).

Downloaded 2026-10-04. The checkout serves these static assets locally; it does not send payment details to the logo hosts.
